Most sights and malls cluster in Futian & Nanshan (near the crossings). Beaches (Dameisha, Xiaomeisha) and old towns are out east; getting there is usually taxi/bus. From LOHAS Park, the easiest entry is the East Rail line to Lo Wu, or the high-speed rail to Futian.
